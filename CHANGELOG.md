# Changelog

## 1.1.1 - 2026-11-20

- **Bags are always picked up**, also when your bags are almost full. A bag
  gives you more room, so it no longer stays on the corpse.

## 1.1.0 - 2026-11-19

- **Minimap button** with the add-on icon: left-click opens the options,
  right-click turns the filter on or off. Drag it to move it around the
  minimap; hide it in the options or with `/fji minimap`.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-18

First release for WoW Forever.

- **Loots for you and leaves junk behind.** Gold, quest items and everything
  white or better are picked up; grey items stay on the corpse unless they
  sell for at least the amount you choose (5 silver by default).
- **Smarter when your bags are almost full.** With only a few free slots
  left, just quest items, green or better items, the materials for your own
  professions and items that fit on a stack you carry are picked up.
- **Ready to skin.** Corpses you can skin, mine or gather from are emptied
  completely, so you can use your profession right away.
- **Chests, herbs, ore and fishing** are always emptied completely.
- **Hold Shift** while looting to take everything.
- **Always loot and Never loot lists**: drag an item onto a list in the
  options.
- **Options window** (`/fji`) with your professions and how many items were
  left behind.
